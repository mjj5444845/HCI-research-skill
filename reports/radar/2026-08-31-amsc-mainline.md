## English executive interpretation

This week changes AMSC's measurement agenda rather than resolving its core empirical gap. SILICA shows that apparent LLM convention formation can be accelerated or shaped by shared label priors and apparatus details; MemUse shows that memory availability and Direct-QA retrieval do not imply natural use of shared history. Two simulations add complementary mechanisms: embodied/ecological constraints can force a referential-code transition, and bodily consequences can make later linguistic policy partner-sensitive. The defensible next contribution is not another diagnostic checklist, but a discriminant-valid embodied human–AI experiment that jointly manipulates shared priors, dyadic history, bodily affordances, repair, and partner transfer.

## 本周论文及其总结分析

### X13 — Must Read: [Benchmarking large language model agent societies against human behavioural distributions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28182)

- **Source:** Raad Bin Tareaf；2026-08-28；arXiv v1，开放 PDF。
- **AMSC fit:** 9.2/10；C/F；Convention → Adaptation。
- **Mechanism and evidence:** SILICA uses five human-anchored social environments, 12 open-weight models, 9,115 model runs, apparatus perturbations, and contamination controls. In its naming game, per-agent label-pool permutation left 7/8 models converging in every seed but 1.5–2.6 times more slowly; an eighth converged in only 2/3 seeds at 14–24 times the fixed-order time. No finding reached the paper's transferable tier; convention formation alone reached its robust tier, and no model matched the human tipping-point bracket.
- **Contribution:** Shared label prior, label order, and interaction-driven negotiation must be causally separated. Convergence alone is insufficient evidence of convention formation.
- **Boundary:** Single-author preprint; human behavior is imported as anchors; the shuffled-pool control was added after transcript audit; all agents still share pretrained label semantics. It does not establish human-equivalent convention, common ground, embodiment, or mutual belief.
- **Gap effect:** GAP-04/07 remain PARTIALLY_ADDRESSED, but a generic “diagnostic components are missing” claim is weaker. The residual is integrated discriminant validity.

### X14 — Important: [The emergence and evolution of a referential code in populations of bee-like agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25779)

- **Source:** Grzegorz Chrupała；2026-08-26；arXiv v1，开放 HTML/PDF。
- **AMSC fit:** 8.4/10；C/G；Meaning → Convention → Adaptation → Embodied Communication。
- **Mechanism and evidence:** A colony-level evolutionary simulation couples directional signals, food ecology, comb geometry, sender rules, and receiver rules. Iconic pointing emerges in an intermediate ecology where signaling is useful and observable. Vertical-comb benefit and sender–receiver mutation coupling permit transition to a gravity-referenced abstract code. In the strongest held-out setting, two transformations stabilized in 91/100 and 92/100 runs without nonviable colonies.
- **Contribution:** Body and ecology can change which code is viable and whether encoder/decoder change can occur without communication collapse.
- **Boundary:** Feasibility simulation, not behavioral probability; across-generation rather than interaction-timescale change; direction-only signal; no human, robot, repair, multimodal integration, or relational meaning.
- **Gap effect:** Adds mechanism-adjacent evidence to GAP-01/03 without changing status. It motivates an embodied-affordance-shift renegotiation experiment.

### X15 — Important: [Synthetic Linguistic Agency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27843)

- **Source:** Sixin Chen, Taizhou Chen；2026-08-28；arXiv v1，开放 HTML/PDF。
- **AMSC fit:** 8.2/10；E/G；Adaptation → Embodied Communication。
- **Mechanism and evidence:** A simulated mortal agent links body state, one of four linguistic actions, partner response, remaining useful life, and social biography in a nonresetting feedback loop. Body state and history alter later policy; person-specific evidence increases correct-action probability by roughly .356–.377 and yields positive remaining-life effects. Reset/non-mortal controls retain general linguistic learning but lose persistent next-turn bodily consequence.
- **Contribution:** Distinguishes remembering interaction from interaction consequences that causally change later communicative policy.
- **Boundary:** Scripted ecology, one-dimensional body, fixed actions, scripted partners, no humans, and no open meaning, repair, or negotiation. Artificial sense-making remains contested.
- **Gap effect:** Supplements GAP-03/04/06 mechanisms but does not establish common ground or convention.

### X16 — Important: [MemUse](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24189)

- **Source:** Ryuichi Sumida, Koji Inoue, Tatsuya Kawahara；EMNLP 2026 Main；2026-08-25；开放全文、corpus、judgments 与 prompts。
- **AMSC fit:** 8.7/10；C/E；Grounding → Adaptation。
- **Mechanism and evidence:** A four-month deployment with 40 users, 1,872 sessions, and seven rotating memory conditions distinguishes prompted recall from natural use of history. Existing-benchmark Direct QA ranges from 19.7% to 70.1% with no satisfaction change. For the same model and context, 78.8% of facts are answerable under Direct QA but only 7.9% appear naturally. Natural Integration, not Direct QA, is associated with satisfaction in observed cue moments.
- **Contribution:** Memory availability → elicited retrieval → natural integration → shared meaning/convention must be separate evidence layers.
- **Boundary:** The Natural Integration–satisfaction analysis is observational and limited to 48 sessions/16 users; explicit cues are sparse; all conditions retain summary context; text-only, with no reciprocity, mutual belief, convention, or embodiment.
- **Gap effect:** GAP-04 is further PARTIALLY_ADDRESSED, not resolved.

## 当前研究现状与 Gap 更新

### 最大可辩护状态变化

1. **Convention convergence 的因果归属更难。** Shared pretrained priors and apparatus rendering can create the appearance of nearly instant convention; negotiation becomes visible only after prior disruption.
2. **Embodiment can be a code-transition mechanism.** The bee simulation gives an ecology/body constraint → viable code → coupled transition mechanism, but it awaits human–AI interaction-timescale validation.
3. **Consequential adaptation differs from stored memory.** X15 provides a body-mediated causal loop; MemUse shows retrievable memory does not imply natural integration. Memory, use, adaptation, and convention must remain separate.
4. **No new evidence establishes human–AI mutual convention.** The strongest studies are AI-agent simulation, evolutionary simulation, simulated embodiment, and text deployment.

## What we know / 我们知道什么

- Rapid LLM naming-game convergence cannot be attributed directly to negotiation; shared priors and apparatus effects are viable alternatives.
- Long-term conversational memory availability, prompted retrieval, and natural integration are distinct.
- In simulation, body/ecology constrains referential code, and bodily consequences can change later partner-sensitive linguistic policy.
- None of this proves embodied human–AI mutual common ground or convention.

## What we do not know / 我们不知道什么

- Whether human–AI dyads form partner-specific, reversible, repairable conventions after shared priors are disrupted.
- Whether humans interpret affordance-driven code change as shared meaning rather than one-sided adaptation.
- Whether memory, natural integration, alignment, success, mutual belief, and convention diagnostics show discriminant validity in one embodied protocol.
- Whether results replicate across models, platforms, cultural practices, partner swaps, and population transfer.
- Whether limited database access and same-day indexing hid a small number of records.

## What should be studied next / 下一步最值得研究什么

Run a preregistered physical/AR repeated-reference study crossing: human–human/human–AI/AI–AI dyads; shared versus individually permuted or novel signal spaces; intact history versus pseudo-dyad; stable embodiment versus controlled affordance shift; same partner versus partner swap/population transfer; and role reversal plus induced breakdown/repair.

Primary outcomes should be preregistered as partner-specific transfer cost, repair latency, mapping retention, role-reversal generalization, natural-integration rate, and independent behavioral evidence of shared meaning, with replication across at least two models or embodied platforms.
