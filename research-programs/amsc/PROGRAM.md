# AMSC Research Program v0.1

## Program Name

**Adaptive Multimodal Social Communication Research Intelligence Workflow**

Short name: **AMSC Research Agent**

Long-term research direction:

> **Adaptive Multimodal Social Communication for Embodied AI**

Research identity:

> **I study how humans and embodied AI agents develop adaptive, socially meaningful multimodal communication through situated interaction.**

Core research question:

> How do socially meaningful multimodal signals become grounded, conventionalized, and adaptive in human–embodied AI interaction?

## Core progression

```text
Informal / Multimodal Expression
        ↓
Social Meaning / Pragmatics
        ↓
Grounding / Common Ground
        ↓
Convention Formation
        ↓
Personalization / Co-adaptation
        ↓
Situated Adaptive Communication
```

Long-term conceptual guardrail:

```text
Meaning
  ↓
Grounding
  ↓
Convention
  ↓
Adaptation
  ↓
Embodied Communication
```

A project that only produces:

> new visual effect -> questionnaire -> warmth increases

is normally **not** a priority research-line project unless it reveals a deeper mechanism, boundary, convention, adaptation process, or socially meaningful phenomenon.

# Relevance Guardrail

For every candidate paper, ask:

1. What communication phenomenon is studied?
2. What signal/modality is involved?
3. What does the signal express?
4. Is meaning fixed, context-dependent, interaction-dependent, or emergent?
5. Does the work model interaction history, repetition, personalization, or adaptation?
6. Does it involve physical environment, embodiment, or situated context?
7. What theory or cognitive/social mechanism is used?
8. Does it materially update the current research gap?

Priority:
- MUST READ
- HIGH
- MEDIUM
- BACKGROUND
- REJECT

Reject papers with only superficial keyword overlap.

## Gap Registry

Every gap has:
- unique ID
- statement
- evidence
- status
- version history
- closest work
- skeptic challenge
- research implication

Statuses:
- UNCHANGED
- STRENGTHENED
- PARTIALLY_ADDRESSED
- REDEFINED
- WEAKENED
- RESOLVED
- REMOVED

High-priority gap requires:
- at least 2 independent evidence sources
- Skeptic challenge

Initial seed gaps:
- GAP-01 Fixed signal effectiveness -> meaning formation
- GAP-02 Designer-defined vocabulary -> emergent convention
- GAP-03 Context-aware -> history-aware communication
- GAP-04 Personalization != convention formation
- GAP-05 Informal visual language lacks systematic embodied communication theory
- GAP-06 Social meaning and functional robot-state communication remain separated

# Temporal Updating

Do not preserve obsolete gap wording forever.

Example:

```text
GAP-03

2026-08:
Context-aware systems exist, but interaction history is rarely modeled.

2026-11:
Several systems now model interaction history, but not shared conventions.

2027-04:
How should agents infer when a stable partner-specific convention has emerged?
```

The system's job is to update its model of the field, not defend old novelty claims.
